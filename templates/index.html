NO STREAKS NO GUILT SCORES NO DASHBOARDS TO MAINTAIN JUST TONIGHT'S ONE THING

Most reminder apps punish you. This one doesn't.

Every habit tracker eventually turns into a scoreboard of your failures.

RE-mindr was built backwards from that — it only ever shows you what you did, never what you missed.

One intention, not a list

You write a single thing you actually want to do — not a to-do list you'll abandon by Wednesday. Simplicity is the point, not a limitation.

Your words, sharpened by AI

You write it messy: "I want to exercise tomorrow." We turn it into the push that actually moves you: short, direct, timed to land exactly when you need it.

Skips disappear. Wins don't.

Tap "Skip" and it's simply gone — no record, no red mark. Tap "Done" and it's saved, ready to be celebrated at the end of your day.

A nudge, not an alarm

The reminder arrives like a text from a friend who believes in you — not a blaring alarm demanding compliance.

Three steps. That's the whole app.

No onboarding flow, no settings to configure before it's useful.

i.

Say it, any time

Write your intention tonight or first thing in the morning — whenever it's actually on your mind. Pick a time to be reminded.

You write "I have an important business meeting tomorrow"
ii.

We push it back, sharper

RE-mindr rewrites it into something built to move you — tone adapts, whether the moment calls for energy or gentleness.

iii.

You choose, honestly

Tap "Done" or "Skip." That's the entire interaction. No streak breaks, no explanations required.

You decide Done → saved to today's completed reminders. Skip → quietly forgotten.

At the end of the day, you only see what you did.

No percentages. No "you missed 3 of 5." Just a quiet, honest look at what you actually followed through on — because that's the only number that builds confidence instead of eroding it.
